Privacy summary: FILENZA is designed to manage files primarily on your device. It has no developer-operated account or file-storage server, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information. Optional cloud, network, PC-connection, search, sharing and feedback features act only when you enable or invoke them.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the FILENZA Android application (“FILENZA,” “the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It does not describe any other application.

2. Information FILENZA can access

Files and storage metadata

Because file management is FILENZA’s core function, the app can request broad storage access. If you grant it, FILENZA may read file and folder names, paths, sizes, types, dates and file contents in accessible storage. It uses that access to browse, search, preview, organize, copy, move, rename, share, delete, recover, archive and otherwise perform actions you request.

FILENZA does not automatically upload your local files to us. A file leaves the device only when you deliberately use a feature such as Share/Open With, a cloud transfer, a configured network transfer, or an authorized PC Connection.

Local indexes and diagnostics

Literal search, optional Intelligent Search, file-health findings, recovery records, operation history, settings and locally generated diagnostics may be stored on your device. When Intelligent Search or content extraction is enabled, supported file text and OCR results are processed and indexed on-device. FILENZA does not implement remote upload of its file index. Secure Space, recovery and protected/private locations are excluded from ordinary content indexing as implemented by the app.

Startup and crash diagnostics are stored locally. They are not automatically sent to us. You can review and copy them before deciding whether to share them.

4. Secure Space

Secure Space encrypts protected files and associated state locally. Your vault PIN and Android Keystore-backed biometric protection are used to control access. Secure Space contents are excluded from ordinary search and sharing while locked. Leaving the app locks the space as implemented by the current build.

FILENZA does not send your vault PIN, encryption keys, raw biometric data or Secure Space contents to us. You are responsible for retaining any PIN or recovery material made available by the app. Deleting app data, uninstalling the app, losing keys or permanently deleting encrypted files may make data unrecoverable.

5. Google Drive

Google Drive connection is optional. Google handles account selection and consent; FILENZA does not request or receive your Google password. The app requests the Google Drive scope necessary to browse and manage the Drive content made available by your authorization. This may allow FILENZA, at your direction, to list metadata, read/download file contents, upload or create items, rename or move items, and send items to Google Drive Trash.

FILENZA uses Google user data only to provide visible file-management functions that you request. It does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, data brokerage or sale. It does not permit humans to read Google user data except when you explicitly share it for support, when required for security or legal compliance, or when otherwise permitted under Google’s policies with appropriate consent.

FILENZA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The returned Google access token is protected locally and used for a short session window. The current implementation does not operate a developer backend for unattended Google refresh credentials. Disconnecting removes locally saved authorization after active transfers finish or are cancelled; revoke FILENZA separately in your Google account if you also want provider-side authorization revoked.

6. Dropbox

Dropbox connection is optional and uses Dropbox’s authorization-code with PKCE process. You approve access on Dropbox’s website and paste the one-time code into FILENZA. FILENZA never asks for your Dropbox password.

Depending on the Dropbox app configuration and your authorization, FILENZA may read account information, list metadata, download file contents, and create, upload, rename, move or delete items at your direction. Dropbox access and refresh credentials, when issued, are protected locally. Disconnecting removes saved credentials from this device but does not itself revoke the provider authorization or delete cloud files.

7. Network Storage and PC Connection

If you configure SMB, SFTP or HTTPS WebDAV, FILENZA connects directly from your device to the host you specify. Connection names, addresses, usernames and credentials may be stored locally; secrets are protected using device-backed encrypted storage where implemented. These details are not sent to a FILENZA-operated server.

Nearby discovery is user initiated and limited to network discovery functions. A discovered device name or address does not grant file access. SFTP requires host-key verification, WebDAV requires trusted HTTPS, and SMB is intended for trusted private networks.

PC Connection starts a temporary, authorized local-network service only when you request it. It exposes the folder and access level you select for the active session. A person or device that has the displayed authorization information may access that session, so use it only on a trusted private network and stop it when finished.

8. Sharing and external applications

When you select Share, Open With, browser authorization, email feedback or another external action, Android or the selected third-party app receives the data required for that action. Its own privacy policy then applies. FILENZA grants temporary file access through Android’s protected sharing mechanism where appropriate.

9. Feedback and support

The Feedback & Support action prepares an email in an email application chosen by you. The draft may contain the app name, app version, approved text-only branding and the comments you enter. Nothing is sent until you review and send it. Diagnostics or files are included only if you deliberately add or paste them. Messages you send are received at pdnexus.apps@gmail.com and retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support history, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations.

10. Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The reviewed FILENZA build contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK. We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use file contents, cloud data, network credentials or diagnostics for targeted advertising.

11. When information may be disclosed

FILENZA sends data to a third party only when necessary for a feature you invoke, such as Google Drive, Dropbox, a server you configure, a device connected through PC Connection, an Android share target, or your email provider. We may also disclose information you send to support when required by law, necessary to protect users and the service, or needed to investigate abuse. We do not operate a general FILENZA cloud database of your files.

12. Retention and deletion

  • Local settings, indexes, histories, recovery records and encrypted connection information remain on the device until you remove them through app controls, clear app data, or uninstall, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.
  • Turning an optional feature off may preserve its settings or saved connection profile so it can be re-enabled later.
  • Use Disconnect to remove a saved cloud authorization from this device. Use the provider’s connected-app settings to revoke provider-side access.
  • Deleting or moving a local, network or cloud file changes the selected storage location. Google Drive deletion uses Drive Trash where supported; other providers and permanent deletion follow their own behavior.
  • Support emails can be deleted on request unless retention is required for security, legal or operational reasons.

13. Security

FILENZA uses Android platform protections, local encryption for sensitive stored credentials, TLS/HTTPS for supported remote services, host verification for SFTP, and authenticated access for relevant connections. No system is perfectly secure. Keep Android updated, use a device lock, verify remote hosts, avoid untrusted networks, protect connection codes and maintain independent backups of important files.

14. Children

FILENZA is a general-purpose file utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated FILENZA service. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information to support may contact us to request deletion.

15. Policy website hosting

This policy page is hosted through GitHub. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy statement when you visit this page. That website hosting is separate from FILENZA’s in-app file processing.

16. Changes

We may update this policy when FILENZA’s features, providers or legal requirements change. The revised page will show a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate app or release channel where required.
